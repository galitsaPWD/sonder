about sonder

the concept

sonder is the realization that every passerby has a life as vivid and complex as your own. this project is a space to notice that.

the intent

a quiet place to leave things behind. poems, memories, songs, moments. nothing here asks for your name, only what the world feels like from where you are standing.

credits

built with leaflet.js, supabase, openstreetmap, cartodb, spotify web api, and a lot of late-night energy.

support the project

sonder is a personal project. if you’d like to help keep the server running or contribute to a future domain, consider buying me a coffee.
